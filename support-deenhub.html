App support and charitable giving

App support goes to the team maintaining DeenHub. It is not a charitable donation and does not provide a charitable tax receipt. The app’s separate Giving area links to charities, which handle their own donations, recurring gifts and receipts.

You stay in control

For future monthly support, you will be able to manage or cancel through Apple subscriptions. Restore purchases will check monthly support; one-time consumable contributions are not restored as benefits. For purchase or refund questions, use Apple’s purchase support or contact us.
