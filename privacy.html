Privacy policy

This policy describes DeenHub for iPhone and Apple Watch, operated by Islamic Apps, powered by Toronto TechWorks.

Last updated: 14 November 2026. Questions and requests: islamicappsdeveloper@gmail.com.

Information saved for you

DeenHub saves prayer records, the selected city and calculation settings, reminder preferences, reading position, Qur’an notes and bookmarks, and dua favourites and saved sets. Prayer records may include a date and your Home or Masjid choice. Reading and prayer information can reveal religious practices.

Reading options and Al-Kahf coverage and completion are saved locally. Eligible Arabic reading exposure can contribute to saved ayah coverage even with reminders off. Opening a chapter or listening alone does not mark it complete. Text geometry and finger-exploration state are transient; this feature does not save a finger-movement history. Coverage is not a measure of understanding.

The prayer journal uses Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit when available and configured. Device backups may also contain app data. The Watch exchanges content, the selected Qur’an reciter, prayer snapshots, queued logging commands and reminder settings with the paired phone. Watch reading notes and favourites are separate from phone notes.

Location, cities and reminders

With your permission, location and compass information support Qibla, nearby places and city suggestions. Apple’s location, geocoding and mapping services may process these requests. Optional background city suggestions need the corresponding iOS permission. DeenHub proposes a city change for you to confirm; it does not silently replace your prayer city.

The app can retain observed coordinates, accuracy and time, resolved city and time zone, pending suggestions and previously kept destinations. Turning suggestions off stops monitoring and clears the pending suggestion, but does not erase all saved location or destination records. Local freshness limits are not deletion deadlines.

Some mosque search routes send the search-area coordinates to DeenHub’s service; city queries send a city and time zone, and timetable requests can identify a mosque. Other catalogue downloads filter on the device. Do not assume that all location-related requests stay on your device.

Reminders use Apple’s notification services. Notifications can show a prayer, city, Al-Kahf or giving reminder on your lock screen according to your settings. The Al-Kahf request identifies the Friday cycle but does not attach your coverage, notes or journal. Turning reminders off does not automatically erase saved reading progress or completion.

Optional app announcements

App announcements are off by default and separate from your prayer reminders. If you enable them in Settings, DeenHub uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service to deliver app news. These services process an app-installation identifier, an Apple delivery token and technical app/device and network information, including your IP address. This integration does not send your prayer journal, Qur’an notes, mosque selections or precise location. DeenHub does not enable Firebase Analytics.

Turning announcements off requests device and Firebase messaging unregistration. If server removal fails, the app retries when it next opens. Notifications already delivered or in transit may remain. Turning announcements off does not delete the Firebase installation identifier or unrelated prayer reminders. See Firebase’s privacy information for its data practices. Contact our support email with privacy or deletion requests.

Content downloads and service providers

DeenHub contacts its Cloudflare-hosted content service for Qur’an resources, dua updates, mosque timetables and signed content-availability rules. Recitation can also contact Quran.com audio services. Previewing, playing or downloading an additional reciter can contact AlQuran Cloud for recording information and Islamic Network for audio. These requests can identify the reciter and chapter or verse; the Watch can make its own requests to these services. Each contacted service can receive the requested URL and ordinary connection information, including an IP address. Content paths can identify a chapter, recording, mosque or regional resource.

Content-update requests do not attach your private notes, saved sets or prayer journal. Optional page-reference downloads retrieve edition indexes, not your finger positions or selected ayah. Content and validation records are cached locally. Expired or withdrawn records may be retained to prevent unsafe reuse; a cache is not an anonymity guarantee.

Your reciter choice is saved on your device and can be shared with the paired Watch. Selecting a name does not itself download a recording; playing a sample is a separate action. Recitation requests do not attach your private notes, bookmarks, prayer journal or saved mosque choices.

Ordinary playback may use temporary audio files that can be removed during cache cleanup. Files in use are protected while needed. Choosing to keep a recording offline saves a separate copy, which you can remove through audio storage controls. Kept audio is excluded from device backups by the app. Temporary cache reuse is not a guarantee that a recording will remain available offline. Content restrictions can still prevent playback of a retained file.

Rights or editorial decisions can restrict content remotely. A device must connect to receive a new decision. A known restriction can remain in effect offline, but we cannot recall material copied outside the app or promise immediate removal from operating-system displays.

This website uses GitHub Pages hosting and contains no embedded analytics, advertising, payment forms or third-party fonts. GitHub receives connection information when you visit. Following an external link subjects your visit to that provider’s privacy practices.

Relevant provider information: Apple, Cloudflare, GitHub, Quran.com, AlQuran Cloud service terms, Islamic Network service terms, and Google.

Journey plans and prayer reminders

Your saved journey can include departure and arrival cities, their coordinates and time zones, itinerary dates and times, travel mode, airport or terminal choices, calculation settings, your separate or combined prayer arrangement, estimated prayer windows and reminder choices. Requesting a journey reminder saves the plan on this device before scheduling; the saved plan can remain even if the reminder cannot be added. A saved driving plan also retains the route geometry and estimated travel time returned by Apple Maps.

When you request a driving estimate, Apple Maps receives the route endpoints and departure time. When you explicitly search for nearby places to pray, Apple Maps receives a mosque search for the chosen area. This planner does not continuously track your journey. Opening a facility link or Maps result contacts that external service under its own privacy practices.

Journey reminders use local Apple notifications. A notification can display the prayer name or combined prayer names on your lock screen. The notification request includes a journey identifier, a prayer-window identifier and the scheduled time; it does not attach your itinerary coordinates, route geometry or private notes. iOS controls notification delivery.

Removing a reminder leaves its saved journey available. Removing a journey also requests removal of that journey’s reminders; regular prayer reminders are separate. Follow any retry message if removal cannot be confirmed. Device backups and already delivered notifications have separate retention and controls.

Support, reports and timetable contributions

Emailing support shares your email address, message and attachments with us and our email provider.

Our optional Google Forms support form collects the email address you enter, the issue category, and any optional name, city/country, app version, message and device type you supply. Google processes the form and stores submitted responses for our review under its privacy practices. Responses are private to our support account; the form does not publish response summaries. New submissions trigger an email notification to our support inbox with a link to the private response. The alert does not include the full submission text. Form responses are separate from the in-app relay; its 14-day inbox rule does not apply to the form. You can request access or deletion through our support email.

In-app reports can be copied, shared to a destination you choose or submitted through the configured DeenHub report service. Reports include your description, an optional correction and screen context that is included by default but can be removed before submission. Diagnostics are optional and off by default; they can include app and OS versions, device family, language and appearance. Your prayer journal and private notes are not automatically attached.

Structured mosque contributions include the mosque identity and coordinates, time zone, dated timetable and expiry, entered times and any evidence link you provide. These are the selected mosque’s coordinates, not a separate device-location reading. Required timetable fields remain when optional screen context is off. Receipt means received for review, not verified or published.

Drafts, pending submissions and receipts can remain on your device for recovery. Deleting a draft does not withdraw a sent or uncertain submission. The report relay is configured to stop serving inbox records after 14 days, with physical cleanup performed by scheduled alarms that can run later. That does not erase material already copied for investigation, email correspondence, provider logs or backups. We retain correspondence and investigation records as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Contact us about access or deletion; include a report receipt if available and avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Giving and optional app support

Charity links open the charity’s own website. The charity handles payments, recurring agreements, receipts and its own privacy practices. DeenHub does not receive confirmation that you donated; its giving reminder does not schedule a payment.

DeenHub supports optional App Store tips and monthly support subscriptions when offered through Apple. Product availability and prices are shown in the app; an unavailable option cannot be purchased. The iOS app uses Apple’s purchase system and does not send you to an external developer-payment checkout. When products are available, Apple handles checkout and maintains purchase records. DeenHub uses product identifiers and verified transaction information, including transaction identifiers, expiry and revocation status, to process support and check subscription status. This support-service state is held in app memory; it is not sent to a DeenHub server or analytics service. DeenHub does not receive your card details. Monthly support renews until cancelled through Apple. App-support payments are separate from charitable donations.

Your choices and retention

You can change location, notifications, lock-screen previews and iCloud permissions in Apple settings; edit prayer records; manage bookmarks and saved sets; and edit or remove individual Qur’an notes. Removing the app may remove its local container, but does not guarantee deletion from iCloud, backups, the paired Watch or submitted reports. Manage those separately and contact support for help. DeenHub does not currently provide a single verified cross-device erase or export control.

We use submitted information to operate features, investigate content issues and respond to you. Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Provider logs and backups have their own retention rules. No network or storage system can guarantee complete security. Avoid including sensitive details in reports unless necessary.

If a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and address it. We may update this policy as features or data practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
