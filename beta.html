Android closed testing

Help shape Deen.

Join our free Android beta in three steps. Use the same Google account for the group and Google Play.

The first release is being prepared for Google review. You can join the group now. The Play opt-in and install link will work once Google makes the test available.

1. Join the tester group

Join Islamic Apps Android Testers and choose Join group. Existing members can skip this step. Google handles your group membership and email preferences; you can leave or change those preferences at any time.

2. Opt in on Google Play

Become a Deen tester. Joining the group alone does not opt you into this app’s test. If the page says unavailable, check your Google account and return once the release is approved.

3. Install and try Deen

After opting in, follow the Google Play download link on that page. Please use Deen normally and share honest feedback. If you can, stay opted in for at least 14 consecutive days to help us meet Google’s production-access requirement. You can leave at any time.

Send feedback or report a bug. Include your device, Android version and what happened. Please do not send private prayer records or unnecessary personal information.

No payment required

Beta membership is free. It does not start a paid subscription. Optional financial support inside Deen is separate and is never required to test or access features.
